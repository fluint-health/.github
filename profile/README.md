# Fluint Health

**AI-powered software for healthcare – so clinical staff can spend more time with patients.**

## About Us

Fluint Health builds software and AI solutions for hospitals and healthcare providers. We combine modern software engineering with a deep understanding of clinical workflows to reduce administrative burden and noticeably simplify everyday processes in the hospital.

## Lunea – Our Platform

Lunea is our AI platform for healthcare. It enables the safe use of AI agents in clinical and administrative practice – embedded in existing hospital infrastructure.

## Core building blocks

- Multi-agent workflows – specialized AI agents that solve complex tasks together
- Declarative workflow engine – define workflows in YAML, version them and execute them traceably
- MCP servers – standardized connection of AI agents to domain data and systems 
- HIS integration – connectivity to hospital information systems
- IHE conformity - healthcare interoperability per design
- Orchestration – process automation AND MORE

## Example Use Cases

- Bed and occupancy management
- Support for coding and billing (DRG / PEPP)
